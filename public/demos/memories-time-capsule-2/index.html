Dear us, twenty years from now

WRITTEN 2016 · OPENED WHEN THE TIME COMES

If you are reading this, the world moved on and so did we. We hope the house in Mumbai still smells of morning chai and that nobody sold the blue sofa.

We were scared of everything back then — jobs, money, growing older. We hope you laugh at that now. We hope you kept the silly traditions and dropped only the grudges.

Most of all, we hope you are still together, still loud at dinner, still arguing about the last slice of cake.

— Mummy, Papa, Ananya & Rohan · 14 February 2016
