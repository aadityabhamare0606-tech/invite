# Our Moments — Future Growth Blueprint

## Brand direction

Our Moments should not stay only a wedding invitation company.

The stronger long-term identity is:

“Beautiful digital experiences for life’s most meaningful moments.”

This allows us to sell:
- invitations
- love stories
- family memories
- milestone celebrations
- keepsakes
- digital archives
- premium event experiences
- partner services for weddings and other occasions

This keeps the emotional, premium, personal brand while expanding beyond weddings.

---

## The core opportunity

The biggest commercial gap is not “more invitation designs.”

The biggest gap is this:

People buy emotional moments, not just event stationery.

They want:
- a beautiful story
- a memorable experience
- a digital keepsake
- a way to share memories
- a premium way to celebrate life events

That is where Our Moments can win.

# 5 more unusual ideas

These are the ones I'd explore because they could make **Our Moments feel different from every invitation website**.

### 11. **Open This Later**

Create a digital message, photo or video that is locked until a chosen future date.
Example: “Open on our 5th anniversary” or “Open when you turn 18.”
**Revenue:** ₹299–₹999 + long-term storage.

### 12. **The Secret Page**

Create a hidden webpage that can only be revealed through a special link, password or scheduled reveal.
Perfect for proposals, surprise birthdays, gifts and anniversary surprises.
**Revenue:** ₹199–₹999 per experience.

### 13. **Everyone Remembers**

After an event, send one link to every guest and ask: **“What do you remember from this day?”**
Collect written memories, voice notes and photographs into one beautiful crowd-created story.
**Revenue:** premium post-event package.

### 14. **Before / After**

Create a story around a transformation: proposal → wedding, old home → new home, childhood → graduation, pregnancy → baby, etc.
The platform automatically presents the journey as a cinematic before-and-after experience.
**Revenue:** ₹499–₹2,999.

### 15. **One Moment, From Everyone**

For one special event, every important person contributes exactly **one photo + one sentence + one voice note**.
Our Moments turns all contributions into a beautiful interactive story of the event.
**Revenue:** ₹999–₹4,999/event.
